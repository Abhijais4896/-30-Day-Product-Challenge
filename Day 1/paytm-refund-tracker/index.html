Product Design Case Study

Paytm UPI • Delayed Refund Experience
The Core Problem
“When a UPI transaction goes wrong, users don't always have enough clarity about what happened to their money, what is currently happening, who is responsible for the next step, and when they can expect resolution.”

The 3 Core User Questions

1
What happened?

Answered in Screen 1 & 2 via explicit transaction status, merchant approval confirmation, and chronological audit timestamps.

2
Where is my money?

Answered in Screen 3 showing inter-bank pipeline state without overpromising, clarifying that Jiomart has already initiated release.

3
What do I need to do?

Answered in Screen 4 & 5 with one-tap zero-friction escalation, eliminating manual copy-pasting of 12-digit UTRs.
